## 📲 Installation

ClipBox isn't on the Play Store, so you install it directly (sideload). It's quick:

1. **Download** the `.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/clipboxstreaming/releases/releases/latest) - do this on your phone, or transfer the file to it.
2. **Open** the downloaded file (check your **Downloads** folder or the download notification).
3. Android will ask to **allow installs from this source** - tap **Settings**, enable **Allow from this source**, then go back.
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open**. Done! 🎉

### Updating

When a new version is out, the app lets you know. Just download the newest APK and install it over the top - your list, history, and settings are kept.

## ✨ Features

- 🎬 **HLS & MP4 playback** with a smooth, modern player (ExoPlayer / Media3)

## ❓ FAQ

**Is it safe to install?**
Yes. ClipBox is a standalone app installed directly from this repository's releases. "Allow from this source" is Android's standard prompt for any app not from the Play Store.

**Why isn't it on the Play Store?**
It's distributed here as a direct download to be in compliance with standards.

**The app says a server or stream failed — what do I do?**
Try another server from the list, or retry. Sources are third-party and can come and go; keeping the app updated helps.

**Casting isn't showing my TV.**
Make sure your phone and TV are on the **same Wi-Fi network**, then reopen the app.
